Choose a day and time in the calendar. Your appointment is final once the office confirms it, usually on the same working day.

## Before your appointment

- For property sales, inheritance or gifts, please email us your documents first, so we can set the date once your file is complete.
- If you do not speak Romanian, tell us when you book: you will need an authorised interpreter at the signing.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment

If you cannot come, please call or email us so we can offer the slot to someone else.
